    Wear Range

    Every weapon skin has a wear value, sometimes also called float value. The wear value determines how scratched the skin looks and also which exterior it belongs to:

    0.07 - 0.15 = Minimal Wear (MW)
    0.15 - 0.38 = Field-Tested (FT)
    0.38 - 0.45 = Well-Worn (WW)
    0.45 - 1.00 = Battle-Scarred (BS)
    Each skin has a minimum and maximum wear value, that's what we call a wear range. The green line shows the available wear range for this skin.

    Description

    Powerful and reliable, the AK-47 is one of the most popular assault rifles in the world. It is most deadly in short, controlled bursts of fire. It has been painted using a carbon fiber hydrographic and a dry-transfer decal of a red pinstripe. Never be afraid to push it to the limit

    History

    The AK-47 | Redline was first introduced to CS2 11 years ago, on February 20th, 2014. It was released as part of the Operation Phoenix Weapon Case, alongside the "The Rise of Operation Phoenix" update. The skin was created by the community designer "emkay".

    Origin

    The AK-47 | Redline can be obtained by opening a Operation Phoenix Weapon Case container. The skin is also part of the The Phoenix Collection.

    Popularity

    With a popularity of 99%, the AK-47 | Redline is currently extremely popular, which makes it one of the most popular items in CS2. This popularity measurement is based on the daily sales volume and the price of the skin.

    Rarity

    The AK-47 | Redline is one of 309 other Rifle skins. The rarity of the skin is Classified, which makes the Redline a very rare drop with an estimated drop chance of only 3.2%.

    Availability

    With a price range between $25.22 and $412.23, the AK-47 | Redline is a pretty affordable skin. Fortunately, it's currently widely available and can be purchased on multiple different markets.

    Versions

    The float value of the AK-47 | Redline ranges from 0.10 to 0.70, which makes the skin available in Minimal Wear, Field-Tested, Well-Worn and Battle-Scarred. For each exterior, there also exists a StatTrak version for the Redline.

    Finish Style

    This AK-47 has been painted in a "Custom Paint Job" style, with the Redline finish. This style enables extremely customized looks in a full range of colors. The skin has been painted in a color scheme of red and black. Different pattern indexes have no effect on the Redline finish. The skin uses the old legacy model.

    Community Sentiment

    The AK-47 | Redline has received over 12.8K votes on CSGOSKINS.GG, leading to an average rating of 4.5 out of 5 stars. The skin is currently also owned by the professional CS2 players krimz, device and fallen.
